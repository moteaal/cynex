Cynex Systems delivers end-to-end engineering and manufacturing solutions for industrial applications, specializing in custom automation, machinery, mechanical components, electrical and control systems, PCB and embedded electronics, and laboratory or training equipment. We bring mechanical, electrical, electronics, mechatronics, and computer engineering together to transform concepts and industrial challenges into reliable, practical solutions—from initial design and prototyping to manufacturing, assembly, automation, testing, and final delivery. Whether you need a custom machine, precision component, control system, prototype, or complete industrial project, Cynex Systems can help turn your idea into reality.
