Custom Parts & CAD Design

At Cynex Systems, we design and develop custom mechanical components based on your specific application, functional requirements, dimensions, materials, and operating conditions. Our engineering team uses professional 3D CAD tools to develop detailed components, assemblies, and manufacturing drawings.














From a basic concept or sketch to a complete production-ready design, we consider functionality, strength, manufacturability, assembly, cost, and future production requirements. Our CAD design services support machinery, automation equipment, robotic systems, mechatronic products, fixtures, tooling, and specialized industrial applications.
Prototyping

We transform engineering concepts and CAD designs into functional physical prototypes that can be tested and evaluated before moving into full production. Prototyping allows our engineers and customers to verify dimensions, fit, movement, functionality, and overall system performance at an early stage.









We can develop prototypes for mechanical components, robotic mechanisms, automation equipment, fixtures, enclosures, and custom products using appropriate manufacturing technologies. Early testing helps identify design improvements, reduce development risks, and create a more reliable and production-ready final solution.
CNC Machining

Cynex Systems provides precision CNC machining for custom mechanical components, prototypes, tooling, fixtures, and production parts. CNC manufacturing enables accurate and repeatable machining of a wide range of materials, including aluminum, steel, stainless steel, engineering plastics, and other suitable materials.










Our machining solutions can support components with complex geometries, precise dimensions, and demanding functional requirements. CNC-machined parts can be integrated into industrial machinery, automation systems, robotic equipment, mechatronic assemblies, custom fixtures, and other engineering applications where accuracy, reliability, and repeatability are important.
Laser Cutting

Our laser cutting capabilities provide accurate, efficient, and repeatable manufacturing of custom sheet and plate components. Laser cutting is suitable for producing brackets, mounting plates, structural components, machine frames, panels, enclosures, guards, and other custom mechanical parts.
















By combining laser cutting with CAD-based engineering and downstream fabrication processes, we can efficiently transform digital designs into accurately manufactured components. This approach is particularly useful for prototypes, custom machinery, automation equipment, robotic systems, and small- to medium-volume production.
3D Printing

Cynex Systems uses 3D printing to rapidly manufacture prototypes, custom components, fixtures, brackets, covers, housings, and other specialized parts. Additive manufacturing allows us to produce complex geometries and customized designs that may be difficult, time-consuming, or expensive to manufacture using traditional processes.














3D printing is particularly valuable during the engineering and product-development stages, allowing designs to be quickly produced, assembled, tested, and modified. It can also be used for low-volume components, tooling, machine accessories, and application-specific parts for robotics, automation, mechatronics, and industrial systems.
Sheet Metal Fabrication

We provide custom sheet metal fabrication for machinery, automation equipment, robotic systems, electrical and mechanical enclosures, machine guards, structural components, and industrial assemblies. Our fabrication process can include precision cutting, bending, forming, joining, and assembly according to the requirements of each application.













Our engineering-driven approach allows sheet metal components to be developed directly from CAD designs and integrated with complete mechanical and mechatronic systems. Whether you require a simple bracket, a protective enclosure, or a complex machine structure, we focus on dimensional accuracy, structural integrity, functionality, and practical manufacturability.
From concept and CAD design to prototype and finished component, Cynex Systems provides integrated mechanical manufacturing solutions for industrial and advanced engineering applications. Our capabilities combine engineering design with modern manufacturing processes to develop custom components and assemblies for mechatronics, automation, robotics, machinery, and industrial systems. By integrating design, prototyping, machining, fabrication, and additive manufacturing, we help customers move efficiently from an initial idea to a functional and production-ready solution.
