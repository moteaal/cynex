Explore our main product categories below to learn more about our motion control components, control systems, and interface solutions.
DC Servo Motors

DC servo motors are essential components in applications requiring precise motion control, accurate positioning, and controlled rotational speed or torque. They are used in a variety of industrial machines, automation equipment, positioning systems, and specialized mechatronic applications. Discover our DC servo motor solutions for projects that require responsive motion, controlled movement, and integration with suitable feedback and drive systems. These motors can be considered for applications such as automated machinery, positioning mechanisms, custom-built equipment, and other systems where accurate motion control is important. Selecting the appropriate motor depends on factors such as load requirements, operating speed, torque, control method, and the characteristics of the overall system. Our product information can help you identify solutions relevant to your engineering and industrial applications.
Stepper Motors and Drivers

Stepper motors and their associated drivers provide controlled, incremental movement and are widely used in applications requiring repeatable positioning and coordinated motion. They are suitable for many automation systems, laboratory equipment, machine tools, positioning stages, and custom-designed mechanical assemblies. The combination of a stepper motor and a compatible driver enables controlled movement based on electrical command signals. Depending on the selected components and system configuration, these solutions can support accurate positioning, adjustable movement profiles, and integration with electronic control units. Explore our Stepper Motors and Drivers category to find components for your motion control requirements. When selecting a suitable solution, important considerations include torque, speed, step resolution, supply voltage, driver compatibility, and the mechanical characteristics of the application.
Control Units

Control units play a central role in the operation of industrial machines, automation systems, and mechatronic equipment. They process control signals, coordinate system functions, and support communication between different electrical, electronic, and mechanical components. At Cynex Systems, the Control Units category is intended for customers seeking control-related products for industrial equipment, automated processes, and specialized engineering projects. Depending on the application, control units may be integrated with motors, drivers, sensors, electrical circuits, operator interfaces, and other system components to achieve the required operating functions. Explore this category to learn more about control solutions relevant to your equipment and project requirements. Choosing the right control unit involves considering the required functions, input and output signals, communication interfaces, operating environment, and compatibility with the rest of the system.
Control Panel Interface

Control panel interfaces provide a practical connection between operators and industrial equipment, allowing users to interact with machines, monitor operating conditions, and manage selected system functions. They are important elements in industrial control panels, automation installations, and custom-built machinery. Depending on the system design, a control panel interface may support operator commands, status indication, parameter adjustment, monitoring, and communication with control units or other electronic devices. Our Control Panel Interface category is intended to help customers explore interface solutions for industrial and mechatronic applications. Proper selection and integration of an interface can improve usability, simplify operation, and support effective interaction between the operator and the machine. Explore this category to identify products relevant to your control panel design, equipment configuration, and industrial automation requirements.
