At Cynex Systems, we provide complete custom mechanical part design and CAD development services, helping customers transform ideas, concepts, sketches, existing components, or engineering requirements into accurate and functional 3D models and manufacturing-ready designs. Our engineering approach focuses on developing parts that are not only visually accurate but also practical to manufacture, assemble, operate, and maintain. Our team can design individual mechanical components, complex assemblies, machine structures, brackets, fixtures, tooling, enclosures, robotic components, and specialized mechanisms according to the specific requirements of each application. We consider critical factors such as dimensions, tolerances, material selection, strength, loading conditions, motion, clearances, assembly requirements, and manufacturing processes during the design stage.

For customers with an existing part or system, we can also support design modifications, component improvements, replacement-part development, and reverse-engineering-based CAD development. This allows an existing component to be redesigned, optimized, or adapted for a new application.
Our custom CAD design services support a wide range of applications, including industrial machinery, automation systems, robotics, mechatronics, manufacturing equipment, custom tooling, fixtures, and specialized engineering systems. By combining mechanical design expertise with our capabilities in manufacturing, automation, robotics, and mechatronics,
Cynex Systems can take a project from
Concept → CAD design → Prototype → Manufacturing → Integration.
Typical capabilities include:
- Custom 3D mechanical part and assembly design
- Manufacturing-ready CAD development
- Concept development and mechanical design
- Design modifications and optimization
- Reverse engineering and replacement-part design
- Fixtures, tooling, brackets, frames, and enclosures
- Robotic and automation component design
- Design for manufacturing and assembly (DFMA)
- Prototype and production-part development
- Engineering drawings and manufacturing documentation
Using professional 3D CAD modeling and engineering design tools, we develop detailed parts and assemblies that can be used directly for prototyping and manufacturing. CAD models can also be used to evaluate component fit, identify interference between moving parts, optimize mechanical layouts, and make design modifications before fabrication begins.
