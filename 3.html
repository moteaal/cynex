At Cynex Systems, we combine engineering expertise, innovative design, and advanced technology to develop reliable, efficient, and intelligent industrial solutions. Our capabilities span mechanical engineering, robotics, motion control, machine vision, electronics, and industrial automation. From concept development and detailed design to system integration and implementation, we transform complex engineering challenges into practical solutions tailored to our clients’ needs.

Machine Design

We provide comprehensive machine design and mechanical engineering services, developing custom machinery, mechanisms, and equipment to meet specific industrial requirements. Our capabilities include concept development, mechanical component design, 3D CAD modeling, engineering analysis, design optimization, and prototype development. We focus on functionality, precision, reliability, maintainability, and manufacturability to deliver equipment that improves productivity and supports long-term operational performance.
Robotic and Mechatronic Systems

We design and integrate robotic and mechatronic systems that combine mechanical structures, electronics, sensors, actuators, and intelligent control. Our expertise includes robotic workcells, automated handling systems, custom mechanisms, electromechanical assemblies, and coordinated multi-component systems. By bringing together multiple engineering disciplines, we develop flexible, precise, and reliable solutions for complex industrial tasks, helping improve process consistency, efficiency, and operational capability.
Servo-Mechanisms

We develop precision motion-control solutions for applications requiring accurate positioning, controlled speed, coordinated movement, and repeatable performance. Our capabilities include servo motor and drive selection, motion-system design, feedback sensor integration, control-loop tuning, and multi-axis motion coordination. We engineer servo-mechanisms tailored to application-specific requirements, supporting high-performance machinery, automated equipment, positioning systems, and other applications where dynamic response and motion accuracy are essential.
Machine Vision and AI

We develop machine vision and AI-based solutions that enable industrial systems to inspect, identify, measure, track, and respond to their surroundings. Our capabilities include camera and lighting selection, image acquisition, image processing, feature detection, automated inspection, object recognition, and vision-guided operations. By integrating vision technologies with intelligent algorithms and industrial control systems, we help improve quality assurance, detect defects, support traceability, and enable more adaptive and data-driven manufacturing processes.
Electronic Circuit Design

We provide electronic design capabilities for industrial equipment, embedded systems, sensing applications, and specialized control solutions. Our work can include circuit architecture, analog and digital circuit design, sensor interface development, signal conditioning, power electronics, microcontroller-based systems, PCB design, and prototype testing. We focus on developing electronic solutions that meet application-specific requirements for performance, reliability, integration, and manufacturability, supporting seamless interaction between hardware, software, and mechanical systems.
Industrial Automation

We design and integrate industrial automation solutions that streamline operations, improve process consistency, and enhance production efficiency. Our capabilities include PLC programming, HMI development, industrial control panels, sensor and actuator integration, automated sequencing, machine control, system networking, and equipment integration. We develop automation architectures tailored to individual processes, from standalone machines to interconnected production systems, with an emphasis on operational reliability, maintainability, safety, and scalability.
