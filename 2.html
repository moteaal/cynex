Plant Analysis Vision System

An AI-powered machine vision system designed to analyze plants and support intelligent agricultural decision-making. Using advanced imaging and artificial intelligence, the system can identify plants, analyze their characteristics, detect visible abnormalities, and monitor growth and overall conditions. Customizable for greenhouses, indoor farms, research facilities, and precision agriculture, it helps enable faster, more consistent, and data-driven plant analysis.

Warehouse Robotics Systems (WRS)


Cynex Systems designs and fabricates custom warehouse robotics solutions to facilitate and accelerate material handling, storage, picking, sorting, and transportation processes. Our capabilities include the development of mobile robots, robotic manipulators, innovative hanging robotic systems, intelligent carriers, and customized mechatronic platforms tailored to specific warehouse requirements. By combining novel hardware designs with AI and machine vision systems, we create intelligent robotic solutions that can navigate, identify and handle materials, optimize workflows, and improve operational efficiency.
Hanger Robots

A novel hanging robotic system designed to transform how automation operates in three-dimensional space. By integrating AI, machine vision, advanced mechatronic engineering, and intelligent control, the Hanger Robot can be mounted on ceilings or walls, overcoming the limitations of traditional floor-based robotic systems. Its innovative design enables one or multiple robots to move within a shared workspace without fixed ground bases, approach tasks from different positions and orientations, and maximize valuable floor space. The system can be customized for warehouses, factories, manufacturing facilities, medical and surgical environments, and other complex applications requiring flexible, compact, and highly accessible robotic automation.
Pick-and-Place Robot

Cynex Systems designs and fabricates custom pick-and-place robotic solutions that combine pneumatic and electromechanical systems, precision mechatronics, AI, and machine vision. Our robots can be equipped with pneumatic grippers, vacuum systems, electric actuators, servo-driven mechanisms, and customized end-effectors to handle a wide range of products. Designed for accuracy, speed, and flexibility, these systems can identify, locate, pick, orient, and place objects efficiently, even in dynamic and complex environments. Each solution can be tailored to the specific requirements of manufacturing, packaging, assembly, sorting, warehousing, and automated material-handling applications.
