Popular Services:
Cynex Systems provides integrated engineering and manufacturing solutions for small and medium-sized industrial applications. We design and build custom automated machines, mechanical components, electrical and control systems, embedded electronics, and specialized laboratory equipment.

From individual components to complete automated systems, we combine mechanical, electrical, electronics, mechatronics, and computer engineering to turn ideas and industrial requirements into practical solutions.

Featured Solutions:

Custom Automation & Machinery

Design and manufacturing of custom automated machines, special-purpose machinery, assembly systems, testing equipment, and industrial automation solutions.

Mechanical Parts Manufacturing

Custom mechanical components manufactured using CNC machining, turning, milling, laser cutting, sheet metal fabrication, and 3D printing.

Electrical & Control Systems

Electrical system design, control panels, machine wiring, sensors, actuators, PLCs, HMIs, and industrial control systems.


PCB & Embedded Control Systems

Custom PCB design, prototyping, assembly, microcontroller-based control boards, embedded systems, and digital control solutions.

Industrial Engineering & Projects

Design and development of customized industrial equipment, prototypes, production systems, and complete engineering projects.




Laboratory & Training Systems

Custom laboratory equipment and industry-inspired training systems based on microcontrollers, PLCs, pneumatics, hydraulics, electronics, and mechatronics.
