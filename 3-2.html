At Cynex Systems, we design and integrate robotic and mechatronic systems that combine mechanical engineering, electronics, sensors, actuators, and intelligent control. We develop customized solutions for industrial manufacturing, agriculture, material handling, municipal applications, and specialized engineering projects. By integrating multiple engineering disciplines, we create systems that improve precision, productivity, flexibility, and operational efficiency while addressing complex automation and motion-control challenges.
Our Robotic and Mechatronic Capabilities

Industrial Robotics and Robotic Systems

We develop robotic solutions for repetitive, precision-based, and complex industrial operations. Applications may include robotic handling, pick-and-place systems, assembly assistance, machine tending, sorting, and automated positioning. We consider robot selection, mechanical design, end-effectors, workspace requirements, and control integration to develop solutions suited to specific tasks and operating environments.

Custom Mechatronic Systems

We design integrated systems that combine mechanical mechanisms, electronic circuits, sensors, actuators, and embedded controllers. Our capabilities include electromechanical assemblies, automated mechanisms, intelligent devices, and customized equipment requiring coordinated mechanical and electronic functions. We focus on reliable operation, precise control, compact design, and effective communication between system components.

Agricultural Robotics and Smart Equipment

We develop robotic and mechatronic concepts for agricultural equipment and operations, including automated handling, dispensing, positioning, monitoring, and crop-related applications. By combining mechanical systems with sensing and control technologies, we can help address repetitive tasks, improve operational consistency, and support more efficient agricultural processes.
Our Engineering Approach

We begin by defining the required functions, operating conditions, performance targets, and integration requirements. We then develop the system architecture, select suitable mechanical and electronic components, design the necessary mechanisms, and establish the control and communication interfaces. Depending on the project scope, our support can include CAD design, prototyping, programming, system integration, testing, and performance optimization.

Throughout development, we consider reliability, maintainability, operational safety, and compatibility with existing equipment. The result is a coordinated engineering solution designed around the needs of the application.


Integrated Solutions for Complex Engineering Challenges

Whether you need a robotic handling system, a custom electromechanical device, an automated agricultural application, or a specialized industrial mechanism, Cynex Systems brings together robotics, mechanical design, electronics, motion control, and automation to help transform complex requirements into practical, integrated engineering solutions.
Automated Material Handling and Positioning

We design systems for moving, transferring, orienting, and positioning materials or products with controlled and repeatable movements. Potential solutions include robotic handling mechanisms, automated transfer units, actuated fixtures, conveyor interfaces, and coordinated positioning systems. These solutions can be adapted to manufacturing, warehousing, agricultural processing, and other material-handling applications.

Sensors, Actuators, and Motion Control

We integrate sensors, electric motors, servo drives, linear actuators, and other control components to achieve accurate movement and responsive system behavior. Our work can include component selection, feedback integration, motion coordination, and control-system development. These capabilities enable machines to respond to measured conditions, follow programmed sequences, and perform tasks with improved repeatability.

Machine Vision and Intelligent Integration

We can combine robotic and mechatronic systems with machine vision, image processing, and AI-based technologies to support object detection, identification, inspection, and vision-guided operations. These integrations can enable equipment to adapt its actions to changing conditions, support automated quality control, and improve the flexibility of industrial processes.
