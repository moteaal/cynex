Cynex Systems develops custom Warehouse Robotics Systems (WRS) that combine mechanical engineering, mechatronics, robotics, AI, machine vision, automation, and intelligent control. Our systems can be designed for warehouses, distribution centers, manufacturing facilities, logistics operations, and other material-handling environments. We are ready to design and manufacture complete robotic systems, from the mechanical structure and robotic platform to sensors, actuators, control systems, vision, navigation, and software integration.
Autonomous Mobile Robot (AMR) System
Cynex Systems can design and manufacture custom Autonomous Mobile Robots (AMRs) for automated transportation of materials, products, bins, totes, components, and equipment throughout warehouses and industrial facilities. AMRs can be designed according to required payload, dimensions, speed, operating environment, floor conditions, and transportation requirements. Our AMR solutions can incorporate LiDAR, cameras, sensors, encoders, IMUs, navigation systems, SLAM, obstacle detection, path planning, and intelligent control to allow robots to move autonomously through dynamic environments. Different platforms can be developed for light material transportation, heavy loads, bin handling, production-line supply, warehouse-to-production transportation, and other applications. We can also integrate AMRs with robotic arms, conveyors, automated storage systems, charging stations, warehouse management systems, and production equipment, creating a complete automated material-handling solution.
Automated Storage & Retrieval Systems (AS/RS)
Cynex Systems designs and develops custom Automated Storage and Retrieval Systems (AS/RS) to improve warehouse storage capacity, material flow, and operational efficiency. Our solutions combine automated storage structures, robotic handling,









conveyors, shuttles, sensors, machine vision, and intelligent control systems to automatically store, locate, retrieve, and deliver products, bins, totes, and pallets. AS/RS solutions can be customized for warehouses, distribution centers, manufacturing facilities, and industrial environments based on available space, storage requirements, payload, and workflow. Cynex Systems is ready to design, manufacture, integrate, and customize AS/RS solutions to meet the specific needs of each customer and facility.
GridShuttle AMRS
Cynex GridShuttle AMRS represents a fully engineered leap forward in high‑density warehouse automation, combining intelligent robotics, precision rail‑guided movement, and real‑time coordination across massive storage grids. Each shuttle robot operates on a reinforced steel lattice above modular bins, using advanced sensors, high‑speed drive systems, and distributed control algorithms to execute rapid retrieval, sorting, and transport tasks with exceptional accuracy. Designed for large fulfillment centers and high‑throughput logistics operations, the GridShuttle AMRS fleet works continuously and collaboratively, scaling effortlessly as inventory demands grow. With robust industrial construction, intelligent routing, and seamless integration into modern warehouse management systems, Cynex Systems delivers a powerful automation platform that increases efficiency, reduces operational costs, and transforms traditional storage environments into intelligent, self‑optimizing infrastructures.
