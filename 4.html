At Cynex Systems, we focus on the design and development of educational equipment, laboratory systems, and practical training solutions that bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and real-world engineering applications. Our approach combines mechanical engineering, electronics, automation, control systems, and embedded technologies to support technical education, vocational training, university laboratories, research facilities, and industrial skills development. We recognize that every educational institution and laboratory has unique objectives. Our engineering philosophy emphasizes customized solutions that align with specific curricula, training requirements, experimental procedures, and available infrastructure. From individual laboratory devices to integrated training platforms, our goal is to provide practical, adaptable, and application-oriented equipment that supports effective learning and experimentation.
Special-Purpose Laboratory Machines
CYNEX Systems supports the development of specialized laboratory machines designed to demonstrate engineering principles, investigate technical phenomena, and perform controlled experiments. Potential applications include mechanical testing equipment, motion-control demonstrators, material-handling experiments, thermal systems, process-control rigs, and customized test benches. Depending on the application, machines can incorporate sensors, actuators, measurement instruments, data acquisition, and computerized control. Each system can be designed around specific experimental objectives, with attention to measurement accuracy, operational safety, accessibility, and ease of use. Customized laboratory machines can help students and researchers investigate system behaviour, evaluate performance, and develop practical engineering skills.

Industry-Inspired Pneumatic Training Systems
Our pneumatic training equipment concept focuses on demonstrating the principles and industrial applications of compressed-air technology through practical, hands-on experimentation. Training systems can incorporate pneumatic cylinders, directional control valves, pressure regulators, air preparation units, flow-control components, tubing, sensors, and electrical control interfaces. Modular configurations can support experiments ranging from basic pneumatic circuits to more advanced electro-pneumatic sequences. Students can explore actuator operation, valve logic, pressure regulation, flow control, sequencing, troubleshooting, and automated machine functions. Industry-inspired exercises can demonstrate how pneumatic systems are applied in manufacturing, packaging, assembly, material handling, and industrial automation. Where required, training platforms can integrate PLCs and sensors to introduce automated control and system diagnostics.

Industry-Inspired Hydraulic Training Systems
Cynex Systems' hydraulic training equipment concept focuses on the practical study of fluid power, hydraulic control, and force transmission in industrial machinery. Potential training platforms can incorporate hydraulic pumps, reservoirs, cylinders, motors, directional valves, pressure-control valves, flow-control components, filters, gauges, and measurement sensors. Depending on the training objectives, systems may support manual, electrical, or PLC-based control. Through practical exercises, learners can investigate pressure and flow relationships, actuator movement, force generation, circuit sequencing, load handling, and system performance. Advanced experiments can introduce proportional control, electro-hydraulic actuation, troubleshooting, and measurement of operating parameters. These systems can help prepare students and technicians for applications in manufacturing, mobile machinery, agricultural equipment, construction, and industrial maintenance. Appropriate guarding, pressure protection, fluid containment, and safe operating procedures are essential to laboratory design.

Microcontroller-Based Training Systems
Our microcontroller-based training equipment concept supports practical education in embedded systems, electronics, programming, and intelligent device development. Training platforms can be designed around suitable microcontroller boards, digital and analogue inputs and outputs, sensors, displays, communication interfaces, motor drivers, and actuator modules. Modular hardware configurations allow learners to progress from fundamental programming exercises to integrated control applications. Practical projects may include temperature monitoring, automated lighting, sensor-based control, motor operation, data acquisition, wireless communication, and Internet of Things (IoT) demonstrations. These systems can help students understand the relationship between electronic hardware and software while developing skills in programming, circuit interfacing, debugging, and system integration. Customized modules can also be developed to support specific courses, laboratory experiments, and applied research projects.
Mechatronics Training Systems
Cynex Systems approaches mechatronics training as the integration of mechanical systems, electronics, sensors, actuators, and intelligent control. Training platforms can demonstrate automated sorting, conveyor handling, pick-and-place mechanisms, servo positioning, robotic movement, pneumatic and hydraulic actuation, and sensor-based process control. Depending on the learning objectives, systems can combine PLCs, microcontrollers, HMI interfaces, and industrial communication technologies. Modular training arrangements can support progressive learning, allowing students to study individual components before integrating them into complete automated systems. Practical exercises can cover system design, control logic, motion coordination, fault diagnosis, commissioning, and performance optimization. These platforms can support engineering education, technical colleges, vocational institutions, and industrial training centres seeking to develop practical competencies relevant to modern manufacturing and automation.

Custom Laboratory Devices & Equipment
Cynex Systems supports the concept of customized laboratory devices and equipment developed around specific educational, experimental, and research requirements. Projects may include specialized test fixtures, measurement instruments, electronic interfaces, experimental apparatus, sensor integration modules, mechanical demonstrators, data acquisition devices, and purpose-built laboratory equipment. The development process can begin with defining experimental objectives and technical requirements, followed by conceptual design, component selection, prototyping, and functional testing. Depending on project scope, mechanical assemblies, electronic circuits, PCBs, embedded controllers, and software interfaces can be integrated into a complete device. Customization allows laboratory equipment to reflect specific curricula, research methods, available space, and budget constraints. The objective is to provide practical tools that facilitate experimentation, improve measurement and control capabilities, and support innovation in engineering education and applied research.

Supporting the Next Generation of Engineers
Educational and laboratory equipment plays a vital role in developing the practical knowledge and technical competencies required by modern industry. By connecting theoretical principles with hands-on experimentation, well-designed training systems help learners understand how engineering technologies operate in real applications. CYNEX Systems aims to support educational institutions, laboratories, research facilities, and technical training centres through application-driven engineering and customized equipment development.
