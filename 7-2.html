Hybrid Stepper Motors

Hybrid stepper motors are widely used in industrial automation and precision positioning applications because they combine accurate incremental movement with good holding torque and reliable performance. Available in different frame sizes, step angles, shaft configurations, and torque ratings, they are suitable for CNC machines, automated production equipment, linear positioning mechanisms, and custom-built machinery. Cynex Systems can help select the appropriate hybrid stepper motor according to the required torque, speed, positioning resolution, mechanical load, and available installation space. We can also evaluate compatible drivers and power supplies to develop an efficient, practical, and cost-effective motion control solution tailored to the specific requirements of each industrial or mechatronic project.
Permanent-Magnet and Compact Stepper Motors

Permanent-magnet stepper motors and compact stepper motor designs can provide controlled incremental movement for applications where size, simplicity, and practical integration are important. Depending on their construction and performance characteristics, they can be suitable for small automation devices, adjustment mechanisms, laboratory instruments, control equipment, and specialized electromechanical assemblies. Cynex Systems can evaluate the suitability of these motors by considering the required torque, operating speed, step resolution, duty cycle, and electrical supply. Where necessary, we can help identify compatible drive electronics and propose customized mounting arrangements or mechanical interfaces to integrate the motor into existing equipment or newly developed devices while keeping the overall design economical.
Closed-Loop Stepper Motors

Closed-loop stepper motors combine a stepper motor with an encoder and compatible control electronics to monitor actual motor movement and respond to certain positioning errors. Compared with conventional open-loop stepper systems, they can provide improved motion monitoring and greater confidence in position control, depending on the system design and operating conditions. These solutions are useful for applications involving variable loads, frequent movement, or demanding positioning requirements, including CNC equipment, automated machinery, and industrial handling systems. Cynex Systems can help assess motor torque, encoder resolution, driver compatibility, and control requirements to determine whether a closed-loop configuration offers practical benefits over a conventional stepper system, balancing performance, reliability, and total project cost.
Stepper Motor Drivers and Customized Motion Systems

Stepper motor drivers control the current supplied to motor windings and determine how the motor responds to step commands, direction signals, and other supported control inputs. Depending on the application, suitable solutions may include microstepping drivers, current-regulated drives, integrated controllers, and systems designed to work with industrial automation equipment. Cynex Systems can help select compatible motors, drivers, power supplies, and control interfaces according to the required motion profile and operating conditions. For specialized projects, our engineering approach can also include customized mechanical assemblies, gearboxes, linear motion mechanisms, electrical circuits, and interfaces with sensors or industrial controllers. Our objective is to recommend a technically appropriate, reliable, and cost-effective solution that meets the practical needs of each customer's machinery, automation system, or mechatronic project.
Bipolar Stepper Motors

Bipolar stepper motors provide precise incremental movement and are widely used in positioning systems, automated machinery, and industrial equipment. They typically offer good torque for their size and require a compatible driver to control winding current and stepping sequences. Cynex Systems can help select suitable motor sizes, torque ratings, and driver configurations according to the application's speed, load, power supply, and positioning requirements, providing a practical and cost-effective solution for industrial and mechatronic projects.
Unipolar Stepper Motors

Unipolar stepper motors use center-tapped windings to simplify certain driving arrangements and can be suitable for applications where straightforward control is important. Although their torque performance may be lower than that of a comparable bipolar motor under some operating conditions, they remain useful in selected positioning and automation applications. Cynex Systems can evaluate whether a unipolar motor is appropriate for a particular project, considering its mechanical load, operating speed, available control electronics, installation constraints, and budget.
