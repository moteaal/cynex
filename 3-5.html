At Cynex Systems, we specialize in application-driven electronic circuit design, developing customized solutions tailored to the specific needs, technical requirements, and operating environments of different industries. Our engineering approach combines electronic design, embedded systems, and hardware integration to deliver practical, reliable, and efficient solutions for industrial automation, agriculture, robotics, machine vision, warehousing, and specialized equipment. We recognize that every application presents unique challenges, from precise sensor measurements and real-time control to power management and communication between electronic devices. Our design philosophy focuses on understanding these requirements and selecting appropriate technologies, components, and circuit architectures to achieve the desired functionality and performance. Whether developing a new electronic product, designing a dedicated control board, or adapting existing hardware to meet new requirements, our objective is to transform engineering concepts into dependable electronic solutions that integrate effectively with complete machines and industrial systems.
Engineering & Design Capabilities
Our electronic engineering scope includes analogue and digital circuit design, sensor interfaces, signal conditioning, microcontroller-based systems, embedded controllers, power management, motor-control electronics, and communication interfaces. Each design is developed according to its intended application, considering electrical performance, reliability, environmental conditions, component availability, and integration requirements. We focus on developing circuits that provide the necessary functionality while maintaining an appropriate balance between performance, complexity, cost, and maintainability. Particular attention can be given to electrical protection, electromagnetic interference, thermal management, and signal integrity to support dependable operation in demanding industrial environments.

PCB Design, Development & Manufacturing
CYNEX Systems' electronic design offering includes Printed Circuit Board (PCB) design and development, supporting the transition from circuit concepts to practical electronic hardware. Our PCB engineering scope can include schematic capture, component selection, component footprint creation or verification, PCB layout, routing, design-rule checking, and optimization for size, manufacturability, electrical performance, and assembly requirements. Depending on project needs, PCB development can also encompass prototype preparation, fabrication, assembly, and functional testing, subject to the company's available manufacturing capabilities and production arrangements. Designs can be prepared for applications ranging from compact embedded electronics to industrial control boards and specialized interface modules. By considering the PCB as an integral part of the complete electronic system, we aim to improve component integration, simplify connections, facilitate troubleshooting, and support future product development.
Customized Solutions for Different Industries
Our engineering approach supports the development of electronic systems for a broad range of applications.
Agriculture & Precision Farming: Electronic controllers and sensor interfaces for irrigation, environmental monitoring, agricultural machinery, automated dosing, and precision-farming equipment.
Industrial Automation & Manufacturing: Control circuits, signal-conditioning modules, machine interfaces, monitoring electronics, and embedded systems for production equipment and industrial processes.
Robotics & Intelligent Machinery: Motor-control interfaces, encoder connections, sensor acquisition, embedded controllers, and communication circuits supporting robotic movement and automated operations.
Machine Vision & AI: Electronic interfaces for cameras, image sensors, illumination systems, embedded processors, and communication between intelligent vision equipment and industrial controllers.
Warehousing & Logistics: Electronic modules for conveyor systems, proximity sensing, equipment monitoring, automated handling, and communication between sensors, controllers, and actuators. Each application requires an appropriate combination of hardware, control functionality, and electrical interfaces. Our design approach emphasizes matching the electronics to the actual operating conditions and requirements of the intended equipment.
From Concept to Functional Hardware

Electronic product development requires more than creating a circuit diagram. It involves understanding system requirements, selecting suitable components, validating electrical behaviour, and ensuring that the final hardware performs as intended. Depending on project scope, the development process may include requirements analysis, circuit architecture, schematic design, simulation, PCB layout, prototype development, and functional verification. Testing can assess electrical characteristics, signal quality, operating temperatures, power consumption, and compatibility with connected equipment. We also consider serviceability, component replacement, documentation, and future modifications. These factors help support long-term maintainability and facilitate integration into larger machines and automation systems.
