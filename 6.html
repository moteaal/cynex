At Cynex Systems, we focus on electronic design and embedded control solutions that connect hardware, software, sensors, and actuators into practical engineering systems. Our capabilities can support the development of application-specific printed circuit boards (PCBs), microcontroller-based systems, custom electronic controllers, and interface circuits for industrial, educational, research, and automation applications. From circuit design and embedded programming to control board development and hardware integration, our approach emphasizes functionality, reliability, and adaptability to project requirements. Solutions can be tailored to specific operating conditions, control requirements, communication protocols, and system interfaces.
PCB Design & Manufacturing

PCB design solutions can cover circuit development, schematic capture, component selection, PCB layout, routing, and design preparation for fabrication. Depending on project requirements, boards can be developed for electronic control, signal conditioning, instrumentation, power management, communication, and embedded applications. Prototyping, fabrication, assembly, and testing can be included according to project scope and available capabilities.



Microcontroller-Based Systems

Microcontroller-based systems combine embedded hardware and software to monitor inputs, process data, and control connected devices. Applications may use platforms such as Arduino-compatible boards, Microchip PIC and AVR devices, ESP32, or STM32, depending on processing needs, connectivity, and project complexity. Solutions can support sensor monitoring, automation, data acquisition, motor control, and communication with external systems.
Custom Electronic Controllers

Custom electronic controllers can be designed around specific operating sequences, control logic, and application requirements. Depending on the project, they may incorporate digital and analogue circuitry, embedded processors, switching devices, communication interfaces, and user controls. Potential applications include equipment control, process monitoring, specialized machinery, laboratory instruments, and automated systems.



Microcontroller-Based Control Boards

Microcontroller-based control boards integrate processing hardware, input/output circuits, power regulation, and communication interfaces on a dedicated PCB. These boards can be tailored to control sensors, motors, relays, actuators, and other electronic devices. Design considerations may include electrical compatibility, signal integrity, protection, programming access, and integration with the wider system.



Sensor & Actuator Interfaces

Sensor and actuator interface circuits enable reliable communication between electronic controllers and physical devices. Solutions may include signal conditioning, analogue and digital input circuits, level shifting, isolation, relay drivers, motor drivers, and interfaces for common communication protocols. These functions help controllers interpret sensor signals and operate connected equipment according to application requirements.
Integrated Engineering for Practical Applications

By bringing PCB development, embedded programming, electronic control, and hardware interfacing together, Cynex Systems aims to support projects from initial concept through design and system integration. Whether developing a dedicated control board, a sensor interface, or an embedded electronic subsystem, solutions can be tailored to the intended application and its technical requirements.
