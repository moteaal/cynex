Cynex Systems delivers industrial‑grade Pick‑and‑Place Robots engineered for solid performance, long‑term reliability, and cost‑effective automation across multiple sectors. Built with reinforced frames, high‑precision servo systems, and adaptable end‑effectors, these robots excel in demanding environments such as extrusion manufacturing, warehouse logistics, agricultural processing, and high‑volume packaging lines. Whether handling delicate produce, transferring heavy components, or performing repetitive sorting tasks, our pick‑and‑place solutions provide consistent accuracy, reduced labor strain, and seamless integration into modern production workflows. Designed to be affordable without compromising durability, Cynex robots empower businesses to scale efficiently and operate with confidence in fast‑paced industrial settings.
Pick‑and‑Place Robot Solutions

1. Precision Handling for Industrial Workflows
Cynex Systems’ Pick‑and‑Place Robots are engineered for high‑accuracy material handling across demanding industrial environments. Built with rigid steel frames, advanced servo control, and optimized motion paths, these robots deliver consistent, repeatable performance for extrusion lines, packaging stations, sorting tasks, and assembly operations. Their robust construction ensures long service life even under continuous operation, making them ideal for factories that require dependable automation without excessive maintenance costs.
Advantages of Customized Pick‑and‑Place Robots:
Customized pick‑and‑place robots from Cynex Systems deliver solid, reliable, and affordable automation tailored to the exact needs of industrial manufacturing, warehouse logistics, and agricultural processing. By adapting each robot’s structure, reach, payload, and end‑effector design, we ensure optimal performance for extrusion lines, sorting stations, packaging workflows, and delicate produce handling. These custom‑built systems provide higher accuracy, smoother cycle times, and long‑term durability compared to generic off‑the‑shelf units, making them ideal for environments that demand continuous operation and minimal downtime.
In warehouses, customized robots integrate seamlessly with existing conveyors, pallet systems, and storage grids, improving throughput and reducing manual labor strain. In agriculture, ruggedized housings, gentle‑grip tooling, and dust‑resistant components allow reliable operation in variable conditions while protecting product quality. Across all sectors, Cynex Systems delivers automation that scales with your business, reduces operational costs, and enhances workflow efficiency.

Our engineering team is fully ready to cooperate with your organization to design, customize, and manufacture pick‑and‑place robots that match your exact technical requirements and operational goals.
2. Affordable Automation for Warehousing & Logistics
Designed with cost‑efficiency in mind, our pick‑and‑place units offer powerful automation capabilities at a price accessible to small and medium‑scale operations. Whether deployed for bin picking, pallet sorting, order fulfillment, or conveyor loading, these robots streamline warehouse workflows while reducing labor strain and operational bottlenecks. Their modular design allows easy integration with existing conveyors, storage grids, and warehouse management systems, enabling businesses to scale automation without expensive infrastructure changes.
3. Versatile Solutions for Agriculture & Outdoor Industries
Cynex Pick‑and‑Place Robots are adaptable beyond traditional manufacturing — offering reliable performance in agricultural sorting, produce handling, seedling placement, and repetitive farm‑processing tasks. With ruggedized components, dust‑resistant housings, and customizable end‑effectors, they maintain accuracy even in challenging environments. This versatility makes them a practical solution for growers, food processors, and outdoor industrial operations seeking durable, low‑maintenance automation that improves throughput and reduces manual workload.
