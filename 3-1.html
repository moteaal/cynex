At Cynex Systems, we provide machine design and mechanical engineering solutions for industrial, agricultural, municipal, and specialized engineering applications. We transform concepts, technical requirements, and operational challenges into practical, reliable, and manufacturable machinery. Our approach combines mechanical design, engineering analysis, and innovative problem-solving to develop equipment that improves productivity, efficiency, safety, and long-term performance.
Our Machine Design Capabilities

Industrial Machinery and Equipment

We design custom machinery, production equipment, assembly stations, fixtures, tooling, and material handling systems for manufacturing and industrial operations. Our focus is on developing efficient mechanical solutions that meet specific production requirements, improve process consistency, and integrate with existing equipment and automation systems.

Agricultural Machinery and Equipment

We develop mechanical solutions for agricultural operations, including specialized farming equipment, crop handling and processing systems, dispensing mechanisms, conveying equipment, and custom attachments. Our designs consider demanding operating conditions, durability, ease of maintenance, and the practical needs of modern agricultural applications.

Municipal, Urban, and Infrastructure Projects

We engineer customized mechanical equipment for municipal services, public infrastructure, environmental applications, and urban operations. Potential applications include waste handling systems, public-space maintenance equipment, material collection and transfer mechanisms, and specialized equipment for city operations. We focus on practical designs that address specific operational challenges and environmental requirements.

Custom Mechanisms and Special-Purpose Machines

We design mechanical mechanisms and specialized machines for applications that require unique functions or cannot be adequately addressed by standard equipment. Our capabilities include linkages, gears, motion mechanisms, lifting and positioning systems, clamping devices, and custom mechanical assemblies tailored to specific movement, force, precision, and performance requirements.

CAD Design, Engineering Analysis, and Prototyping

We support machine development from initial concept to detailed mechanical design using 3D CAD modeling, assembly design, engineering drawings, component selection, and design optimization. Where required, engineering calculations and simulation can help evaluate structural strength, motion, and mechanical performance. We can also support prototype development and design refinement before manufacturing or implementation.

Machine Improvement and System Integration

We help improve existing machinery through mechanical redesign, performance optimization, component replacement, and functional upgrades. Our multidisciplinary capabilities also allow mechanical systems to be integrated with motors, servo-mechanisms, sensors, electronic circuits, robotics, machine vision, and industrial automation to create coordinated and intelligent equipment.

Our Engineering Approach

Every project begins with understanding the application, defining technical requirements, and identifying the most suitable engineering solution. We develop concepts, refine mechanical designs, and consider manufacturability, reliability, maintenance, cost, and operating conditions throughout the process. Depending on project scope, our support can extend to prototype validation, equipment integration, and implementation.

Customized Solutions for Your Next Project

Whether you are developing a new industrial machine, improving agricultural equipment, addressing a municipal engineering challenge, or creating a specialized mechanical product, Cynex Systems combines practical engineering and innovative design to help turn your ideas into functional solutions tailored to your needs.
